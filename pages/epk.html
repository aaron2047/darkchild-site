Long Bio · 260 words

Philly's Own,
Since 1999

Anissa Bane has been making Philadelphia laugh since 1999. Working as Ms. Darkchild — the Princess of Comedy — she has built a career now running more than 25 years as a stand-up comedian, host, actress and producer, and is the founder of Absolute Soul Entertainment LLC.

Her start came at the Saffron House of Comedy, where a weekly host segment turned into a stand-up career. By 2001 she had folded hosting into the act, growing into the full multilateral entertainer she is today — a performer who can work a room clean in the afternoon and take the same crowd somewhere else entirely by the evening show.

Since 2012 she has shared bills with Michael Blackson, Cocoa Brown, Rodney Perry, Damon Williams, Rob Stapleton and Tommy 2 Smooth, and worked stages including The Colonial Theatre in Phoenixville and Cannstatter Ballroom in Philadelphia, alongside long-running showcases Women of Comedy, Soul Jam and Juke Sistas. In 2026 she was featured on FOX 29 Philadelphia.

On screen she appeared as Renee Brown in "The Whispering Bench." She is currently developing an original stage play, "Tenant Eviction," and produces her own live events under Absolute Soul Entertainment — including "Days of Our Lives — Comedy Edition," a live recording taping August 16 at Cannstatter Ballroom that pairs a clean afternoon set with an unclean evening show and karaoke comedy.

Ms. Darkchild books as a headliner, a feature and a host, and works corporate events, private parties, expos and community shows alongside club and theatre dates. Her merch line, Walking in My Season, runs at her shows and online.
